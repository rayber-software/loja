Royal Solitaire (Paciência Real) · Last updated September 27, 2026

1. Who we are

The Royal Solitaire app (called Paciência Real in Portuguese) is made by RayBer Software. This policy explains which data the app uses and who it is shared with.

2. Data the app keeps on your device

The game saves only on your device:

  • settings (game mode, sound, theme, language, etc.);
  • game statistics;
  • whether you bought the ad removal;
  • a game counter and the time of the last full-screen ad, used only to decide when to show ads.

We do not ask for your name, e-mail, account or sign-up.

3. Ads (AdMob)

If you have not removed the ads, the app shows a banner and, from time to time, a full-screen ad between games, both from Google AdMob. Google may use the Android Advertising ID to show ads and measure their performance. This follows Google's policy and is not sent to RayBer's servers.

You can limit personalized ads in your device settings (Google > Ads).

4. In-app purchases

The “Remove ads” purchase is processed by Google Play. RayBer does not receive or store your card details. Google Play tells the app whether the purchase was completed, so the ads can be turned off.

5. Permissions

  • Internet — to load ads and check purchases.
  • Advertising ID — used by AdMob.

6. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated.

8. Contact

Privacy questions: please use the RayBer Software developer e-mail shown on Google Play.

Privacy Policy

Sudoku Odyssey · Updated October 18, 2026

1. Who we are

The game Sudoku Odyssey is developed by RayBer Software. This policy describes what data the app uses and who it is shared with.

2. Summary

  • We do not ask for your name, email, account or sign-up.
  • Your game progress is saved only on your device.
  • The app shows Google AdMob ads: an occasional full-screen ad and optional rewarded ads to earn hints.
  • RayBer Software does not receive or store your personal data.

3. Data the app stores on your device

So you do not lose your progress, the game saves only on your device:

  • completed levels, stars, scores and records;
  • Daily challenges and your play streak;
  • the game in progress, so you can resume it;
  • settings (language, music, sound effects and vibration).

This data is not sent to RayBer. It is deleted if you uninstall the game or clear the app data in Android settings.

4. Ads (Google AdMob)

The app uses Google AdMob to show interstitial ads (full screen, between games) and rewarded ads, which only appear when you choose to watch one to earn a hint. To show and measure these ads, Google may collect and process data such as the Android Advertising ID, device information, IP address (approximate location) and ad interactions. This processing follows Google's policy and does not go through RayBer servers.

  • How Google uses data from partner apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • You can reset your advertising ID or turn off personalized ads in Android Settings > Google > Ads.

5. Consent (Europe and UK)

Where the law requires it, the app shows a Google consent form before loading ads. You can change your choice at any time in the game's settings, under ads and privacy preferences.

6. Permissions

  • Internet and network state — to load ads.
  • Advertising ID — used by AdMob.
  • Basic phone state — added by Google's ads SDK; it does not give access to calls, contacts or your phone number.
  • Vibration — play feedback (can be turned off in the game settings).

The app does not request precise location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.

7. Children

The game is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated.

9. Contact

Privacy questions: use the email of the RayBer Software developer account on Google Play.

Privacy Policy

Infinite Dive · Updated on September 27, 2026 (v3, Google Play Games)

1. Who we are

Infinite Dive (Mergulho Infinito in Portuguese) is developed by RayBer Software. This policy describes which data the app uses and who it is shared with.

2. Summary

  • We don't ask for your name, email, account or sign-up.
  • Your game progress is saved on your device and, if you use Google Play Games, also in your Google account (rankings, achievements and backup).
  • The app shows Google AdMob ads only when you choose to watch one (to continue a run or double your coins).
  • RayBer Software does not receive or store any of your personal data.

3. Data the app keeps on your device

So you don't lose your progress, the game saves only on your device:

  • records and statistics for each difficulty level;
  • coins, purchased characters and the character in use;
  • progress of daily missions and the daily reward;
  • personal ranking (your 10 best runs) and the Daily Dive;
  • settings (language, music, sound effects, vibration and sensitivity).

This data is not sent to RayBer. It is deleted if you uninstall the game or clear the app's data in the Android settings.

4. Ads (Google AdMob)

The app uses Google AdMob to show rewarded ads, which only appear when you tap "Continue" or "Double coins". To show and measure these ads, Google may collect and process data such as the Android Advertising ID, device information, IP address (approximate location) and interactions with the ads. This processing follows Google's policy and does not go through RayBer's servers.

  • How Google uses data from partner apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in Android Settings > Google > Ads.

5. Google Play Games (optional)

If you are signed in to Google Play Games on your phone, the game uses this Google service for the world ranking, achievements and to keep a copy of your progress (records, coins, characters and personal ranking) in your account, so you don't lose anything when you change phones. For this, Google uses your Play Games player profile (gamer name, avatar and player ID), and your depth on the rankings is visible to other players according to your Play Games privacy settings.

  • This data stays with Google and does not go through RayBer's servers.
  • You can sign out, delete your game data or change your profile visibility in the Google Play Games app or at play.google.com/games.
  • Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

6. Consent (Europe and United Kingdom)

In regions where the law requires it, the app shows a Google consent form before loading ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Ad and privacy preferences, inside the game.

7. Permissions

  • Internet and network state — to load ads.
  • Advertising ID — used by AdMob.
  • Basic phone state — added by Google's ads SDK; it gives no access to calls, contacts or your phone number.
  • Vibration — the phone vibrates on crashes, coins and power-ups (can be turned off in the game settings).

The app does not ask for precise location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.

8. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children. Ads are set to content suitable for the "PG" (parental guidance) rating.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated.

10. Contact

Privacy questions: use the email of the RayBer Software developer account on Google Play.
